Nightfall browser-demo sample audio

The browser demo packages audio for each timeline in its sample show:

- lofi.mp3 and rap.mp3 are the Nightfall sample tracks, copied from
  crates/app/assets/sample-audio when available.
- When a sample track is unavailable, scripts/generate-browser-demo-audio.mjs
  writes a deterministic synthesized click track at that timeline's audio path
  instead.
